QUIET GLASS — YASIN SARVEGHAD / CONCEPT 01

This is a separate design concept. No existing website source was modified.

REFERENCE SYNTHESIS
Dream OS and the smart-home reference: spacious composition, compact navigation,
and clear divisions between image-led content and quiet controls.
Share popover: floating, rounded surface; internal selection pills; gentle shadow.
The coloured button and diagram references: restrained blue, violet and peach
light behind pale surfaces; soft illuminated edges.
Status buttons: semantic green / blue / orange rather than colour everywhere.
The graph and icon references: consistent outline icons and weight-led hierarchy.

SITE DIRECTION
Retain Home, About, Work, Services, Store, Blog/Notes and Contact as destinations.
Rework the fixed sidebar into a floating rail with visible short labels.
Keep product filters, grid/list, detail previews, saved products, theme, search,
contact and footer. The website board is a first-viewport Store proposal and
shows two product specimens; filters demonstrate the alternative product types.
The sample CTA and search open isolated surface studies; no data is submitted.
Mobile dock in the component board is a visual direction; a final mobile menu
would retain access to every destination.

MATERIAL
Warm/cool neutral pearl canvas #F8F9FB, graphite text #25282F.
Full glass treatment on floating navigation, popovers and selected controls.
Content is supported by a quieter matte surface. Product images retain depth.
Thin optical edges, layered low-opacity shadows, generous 20–28px corners.
Localized blue/violet/peach reflections, without a full-page coloured wash.
The browser implementation would approximate the material with layered CSS;
the concept does not claim native Apple optical refraction.

TYPE
Actual supplied SF Pro Display. Display 60–64px / Medium 500, body 14–16px /
Regular 400. Clear small labels with restrained, size-aware letter spacing.
Before production, small-text and border contrast would be validated across
light/dark and high-contrast settings; the current board is an aesthetic study.

MOTION PROPOSAL
Press 120–160ms. Hover 160–180ms. Surface transitions 240–300ms.
Responsive, interruptible and critically damped. No ambient animation loops.
Transform and opacity carry motion; reduced-motion and reduced-transparency
equivalents remain part of implementation. Static boards cannot verify feel.
The HTML concept includes a few hover, press, filter and native dialog specimens.

REVIEW
Confirm the overall direction, then implementation can be applied to the site.
Focus feedback on: glass amount, colour amount, roundness, type weight and
the floating navigation rail. Existing site remains unchanged until approval.
